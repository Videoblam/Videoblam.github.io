Privacy policy

Videoblam Publishing Pipeline
Last updated: 26 September 2026

This policy explains what the Videoblam Publishing Pipeline does with data. It applies to anyone who authorises or operates the tool. By authorising the tool against a Google account, you agree to this policy.

1. We use YouTube API Services

The Videoblam Publishing Pipeline uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also agreeing to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

2. What the tool accesses

The tool authorises against a single Google account — the one that owns the YouTube channel we publish to — using Google OAuth 2.0. It requests two scopes and no others:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload — to upload video files to our own channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube — to set metadata on those uploads (title, description, tags, category, thumbnail, playlist, schedule, made-for-kids declaration, altered-content disclosure), to manage our own channel's playlists and settings, and to read our own videos back to confirm an upload succeeded.

Within those scopes the tool acts only on the channel it is authorised against. It does not read, collect, aggregate, store or redistribute any other party's YouTube data. It runs no search or discovery queries. It collects no data about viewers, subscribers or commenters. It derives no metrics from YouTube data.

3. What is stored, and for how long

  • An OAuth refresh token and client credentials. Held encrypted in our secrets store so the tool can obtain short-lived access tokens without re-prompting. Never written to source control, logs, or plain files.
  • Episode metadata we authored ourselves — titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails — kept in our own production files. This is our own content, not data obtained from YouTube.
  • Video ids and upload status returned by the API, kept only to confirm an upload succeeded and to correct it if it did not. These are discarded or overwritten on the next run and are not retained beyond 30 days.

No YouTube API data is stored for any other purpose, and none is stored longer than needed for the upload it belongs to. Revoking authorisation (section 6) causes the stored refresh token to be deleted.

4. What we share

Nothing. YouTube API data accessed by the tool is not sold, shared, transferred or disclosed to any third party, and is not used for advertising, profiling or training any model. The only parties with access are the operators of the channel it is authorised against.

5. Third-party content, advertising and tracking

These pages are static. They set no cookies, run no analytics, embed no YouTube player, and perform no device-level tracking of any kind. They host no advertising and no third-party content.

We do not sell advertising or sponsorship on or within YouTube video content or the embedded YouTube player. Our channel may earn revenue through the YouTube Partner Program, which is YouTube's own monetisation of content on its platform.

6. Revoking access

You can revoke the tool's access to your Google account at any time, without asking us, at Google security settings, Third-party apps and services. Revocation takes effect immediately and the tool cannot obtain further access tokens. Ask us at the address in section 8 to delete the stored refresh token as well, and we will confirm when it is gone.

Google's handling of data collected through its APIs is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Children

The tool is not directed at children and is operated only by the channel's owner. We declare the made-for-kids status of every video we publish.

8. Contact

Privacy questions, complaints, or a request to delete stored credentials: contact address pending publication. We answer within 30 days.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and, where the change affects an authorised account, notify the account holder directly.
