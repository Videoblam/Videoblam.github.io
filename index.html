Videoblam

Videoblam produces and publishes video for its own YouTube channel.

Videoblam Publishing Pipeline is our internal tool for publishing that video. It uses YouTube API Services to upload finished episodes to the YouTube channel we own and to set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, playlists and publication schedule. It is operated only by us, acts only on our own channel, and is not offered to anyone else.
